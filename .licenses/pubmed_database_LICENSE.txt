1. Copyright and Bibliographic Data Ownership

- However, individual abstracts and full-text articles may be copyrighted by the respective publishers, societies, or authors. Users must abide by copyright notices and publisher terms for reproduction or redistribution of full abstracts or articles beyond fair use/research citation.

2. Medical Disclaimer
- Information retrieved from PubMed and NCBI databases is for research, educational, and informational purposes only and does not constitute medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment recommendations.
- NLM does not independently verify the clinical accuracy or validity of individual published manuscripts or automated tools citing them.
