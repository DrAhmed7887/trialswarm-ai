## 3. Official Judging Rubric (Weighted Focus)

1. **Technical Feasibility:** Does it actually work? (Live hosted demo, reproducible code, green CI, real API calls, zero mock smoke-and-mirrors).
2. **Innovation & Novelty:** Is the approach genuinely new, leveraging modern multi-agent systems and frontier Gemini capabilities?
3. **Real-World Applicability:** Would enterprise/clinical/developer users genuinely adopt this on Monday?
4. **Market Potential & Fundability:** Is this a venture-backable product worthy of a $500K–$5M seed check?
5. **Go-to-Market Traction & Presentation:** Public YouTube demo video, clear elevator pitch, social engagement.
